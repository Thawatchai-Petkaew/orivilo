# ORV-192 row 9: focus state options

Status: **options for the owner to choose; nothing is built.** Source: Jira ORV-192 comment 10393 (row 9). The owner rejects today's focus state: on a focused Input in dark mode the field's own border turns light, then a gap, then a light ring, which reads as a double outline. This file gives three options (A, B, C) with exact values, measured contrast, the list of every recipe that draws focus, and the designer's recommendation (B). The builder builds only the option the owner picks, from the table of that option.

Rules this keeps (`.claude/rules/design-system.md`, `DESIGN.md` sections 3.1, 7 and 8): focus is its own semantic (`focus.ring`, ink), separate from selection (violet), error (red) and the brand accent (the profile's action colour); no profile may override `focus.*`; every pair is measured by script, not by eye.

### Why B (recommended)

- It removes the double line where the owner saw it. A focused field shows one 2 px ink edge, which in dark mode reads as one light line with the field's own grey border as a faint rim (`/tmp/orv-192-options/focus/B-neutral-dark-en-input.png`). A still shows two lines in dark mode: the grey field border (#85838F) and, 2 px out, the light ring (`A-neutral-dark-en-input.png`); calmer than today, but the owner may still read it double.
- Buttons, checkbox, tabs, tag and tile keep today's look, which the owner did not reject. A ring with a gap is the only form that reads on any fill: on a primary button the ink ring touches the brand fill at 1.42:1 (professional light) to 2.61:1 (neutral dark), so an inside ring would vanish without C's extra line.
- The error border stays red outside the focus band, so error and focus show at once and neither replaces the other (today and A keep the red border too, because the invalid rule comes after the focus rule).
- A field's focus can no longer be clipped by a scroll box, which is where the bleed workarounds (ORV-190, dialog and sheet bodies) came from.
- Trade-off: two focus forms exist (inside for fields, outside for the rest). They read as one system because the colour, width and the "never removed" rule are the same; Primer and macOS use the same split.

C is the most uniform, but it changes every focused control the owner did not complain about, needs a new shadow token, and a primary button's indicator becomes a light line rather than the ink ring.

### WCAG 2.2 by criterion

A note on numbering: in the final WCAG 2.2, 2.4.11 is Focus Not Obscured (Minimum, AA); the 3:1 test sits in 2.4.13 Focus Appearance (AAA: an indicator at least as large as a 2 px perimeter whose pixels change by at least 3:1 between unfocused and focused) and in 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA: 3:1 against adjacent colours). ## Rendered

Method: Storybook served from this worktree on `http://localhost:6400`, each story opened in its iframe with the `mode`, `profile` and `locale` globals, each option's CSS injected with `page.addStyleTag` (`/tmp/orv-192-options/scripts/options.mjs`, export `FOCUS`), the control focused by keyboard (a Tab key press, then `focus()`, then `:focus-visible` confirmed), Playwright 1.56.1 Chromium at device scale 2 with reduced motion (`/tmp/orv-192-options/scripts/render-focus.mjs`). Controls: Input, Input invalid, Textarea, Select, Button primary, Button secondary, Checkbox, Tabs (the selected tab), Block library tile, Inspector `Selected` (its selected tab). Combinations: neutral and professional, light and dark, English and Thai; friendly is measured in the tables above.

- Contact sheet, every combination, columns Today, A, B, C: `/tmp/orv-192-options/focus-contact-sheet.png`
- One sheet per combination: `/tmp/orv-192-options/focus-sheet-neutral-light-en.png`, `-neutral-light-th.png`, `-neutral-dark-en.png`, `-neutral-dark-th.png`, `-professional-light-en.png`, `-professional-light-th.png`, `-professional-dark-en.png`, `-professional-dark-th.png` (same folder)
- Single crops: `/tmp/orv-192-options/focus/<option>-<profile>-<mode>-<locale>-<control>.png`

What the renders show:

- Today: every field shows two lines of focus colour with a band between; in dark mode both are light and of near weight.
- A: the field keeps its grey edge and the ink ring floats 2 px out; in light mode it reads as one ring, in dark mode the grey edge still shows as a second, thinner line.
- B: a field shows one 2 px edge; on the invalid field the red edge stays outside the ink band, and the error message sits under it untouched. Buttons, checkbox, tabs, tile look as today.
- C: fields as B; buttons show the ring hugging their edge, the primary with a light line inside; the tile's dashed edge is covered by the ring; tabs and the inspector tab are unchanged.
- The crops for Select and Textarea show a sliver of the field label above at the top edge, and Button secondary a sliver of its neighbour's edge: crop artefacts of the 12 px margin, not part of the look.
