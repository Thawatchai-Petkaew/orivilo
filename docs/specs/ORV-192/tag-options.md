# ORV-192 row 13: Tag style options

Status: **options for the owner to choose; nothing is built.** Source: Jira ORV-192 comment 10397 (row 13). The owner wants several Tag styles to choose from. Today's tag is an outlined pill with a light border, medium-weight text and a remove x. This file gives four styles (A, B, C, D) with exact values, measured contrast, renders, and the designer's recommendation (A). The builder builds only the style the owner picks.

Rules every style keeps (`.claude/rules/design-system.md`, `DESIGN.md` section 7, catalog `base/tag`): selected is shown by more than colour (the check, a 2 px `selection.fg` edge and the `surface.selected` ground, the same in every style); disabled uses `surface.disabled` and `text.disabled`; remove is its own button with a 24 px wide target (WCAG 2.5.8; at sm the target is 24 x 22 inside the 24 px tag, today and in every style, 2 px short of 24 x 24 next to the selectable label's own target, a gap that only a 26 px sm tag would close); the label truncates on one line with the full text in `title`; focus is the inside ring of row 9. The tag's colours never take the brand accent.

### Why A (recommended)

- It answers the complaint directly: the light frame and the heavier text go, the chip keeps its shape through its fill and a hairline, and the label reads at body weight (400), the same as the text around it.
- It keeps the profile's shape language: a pill in neutral and friendly, 6 px in professional, so a profile still changes the tag's look the way it changes buttons and fields.
- The remove becomes a round button inside the pill instead of a cut-off square segment, and its focus ring is a full circle (Today's is clipped at the pill's end).
- Selected stands out more than today, because the resting tag no longer has a strong edge to compete with the 2 px violet one.
- Trade-off, measured: on the light canvas the fill differs from the ground by only 1.03:1 (neutral) to 1.11:1 and the hairline by 1.18 to 1.30, so on the canvas the tag reads as text with an x on a faint chip (neutral light renders, A column). On a card the chip reads clearly (1.13 to 1.20 fill, plus the hairline), and in dark mode the near-black fill reads on every ground. The label and the x pass on every ground, so this is a look trade-off, not an access failure (WCAG 1.4.11 does not require a boundary for a control its text identifies). If the owner wants a visible edge on every ground, B is the fallback.

B is the safest everywhere (a 3.33:1 or better edge on every ground) but it is still an outlined tag, which is what the owner moved away from. C adds a divider that makes the tag busier and wider than it needs to be. D is the strongest mark and suits one or two chosen filters, not a list of references; its selected state reads quieter than its rest state, and its focus has to change colour.

## Rendered

Method: Storybook served from this worktree on `http://localhost:6400`, the Tag stories opened in their iframe with the `mode`, `profile` and `locale` globals, the English label set through story args ("Logo round 2") or the label text ("Brand A" for the selectable story, whose label is fixed in its render), each style's CSS injected with `page.addStyleTag` (`/tmp/orv-192-options/scripts/options.mjs`, export `TAG`), Playwright 1.56.1 Chromium at device scale 2 with reduced motion (`/tmp/orv-192-options/scripts/render-tag.mjs`). Rows: plain md, removable md, removable sm and md side by side, selectable not selected, selected and removable, disabled, remove focused by keyboard, selectable focused by keyboard, sm and md on a card ground, long Thai and long Latin labels at 360. Combinations: neutral and professional, light and dark, English and Thai; friendly is measured in the table.

- Contact sheet, every combination, columns Today, A, B, C, D: `/tmp/orv-192-options/tag-contact-sheet.png`
- One sheet per combination: `/tmp/orv-192-options/tag-sheet-neutral-light-en.png`, `-neutral-light-th.png`, `-neutral-dark-en.png`, `-neutral-dark-th.png`, `-professional-light-en.png`, `-professional-light-th.png`, `-professional-dark-en.png`, `-professional-dark-th.png` (same folder)
- Single crops: `/tmp/orv-192-options/tag/<style>-<profile>-<mode>-<locale>-<state>.png`

What the renders show:

- Today: in dark mode a light ring around a near-black chip; the selectable tag's focus ring is cut at the pill's start.
- A: a soft grey chip on a card, a near-black chip in dark mode, a faint chip on the light canvas; the x sits in its own circle and its focus ring is a full circle; selected is the clearest of the five because nothing else on the tag has an edge.
- B: crisp white rectangles with a grey edge on every ground; the most legible frame, and the most "form-like".
- C: the grey label segment and the white remove segment read as two parts; at sm the divider crowds the x.
- D: black chips (white in dark mode) that dominate the row; the selected tag looks lighter, so quieter, than the unselected ones next to it.
