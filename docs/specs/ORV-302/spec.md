## Why

ORV-126 sets phase 1 as a CI guidebook: a brand concept, a logo and how it was derived, brand colours and typography, each with its reasons (ORV-126 "Why"). Node 21 is the last step of the fill order: a deterministic assembly of every node's record into `foundation` and `visual` sections (D4), the `unconfirmed` list of skipped strategy (D1), the open items and the nodes the guidebook does not hold, each with a reason. Published guides run from a brand foundation (purpose, positioning, personality) to the visual kit, and guidelines are the last deliverable of identity work (K-0043, 0.75, the source ORV-126 names for node 21).

The board now holds nine of the thirteen POC nodes on staging, and three more are in build. A person can open each node one at a time; nothing yet shows the brand as one document, in order, with what is settled, what is open and what rests on a step they skipped. This story builds that document from what the board already computes, so the guidebook can never say anything the board does not.

ORV-126 planned the guidebook as `assembleGuidebook(state)` over a planned node state (slices 1 to 3: `BRAND_GRAPH`, `classifyEntry`, `planFill`). Those slices were never built; the board was built instead, one story per node, as one view per saved project (`toBusinessView`). This story assembles the guidebook from that view ("Departures from ORV-126").

This story is not a guard on a tool call, so the guard frame does not apply.

### What is real, rule-based, hypothesis-grade and simulated

- **Real (once built):** the page, the assembly from the stored entries of one saved project, the link from the board and back, the access states and the one not-found answer.
- **Rule-based, not AI:** the assembly itself (`guidebook-assembly-1`), every section, order, state, mark and list on the page. It calls no model. The page says so once (`business.guidebook.byRule`). Each item keeps the method of the node that made it: given or written by the person, or worked out by a fixed rule with its rule id.
- **Hypothesis-grade, and labelled as each node labels it:** the five personality scales (ORV-126 D6, no source); ORV-20's colour thresholds and candidate families (engine entries at 0.4); the long-name threshold of the logo type (no source); any link from personality to a typeface, which no rule makes (`guidebook-item-1`, column "Hypothesis label").
- **Drafted by AI, only after ORV-292 ships, and labelled as the board labels it:** a positioning statement the person picked or edited from drafted options (open question 10). Nothing else in the guidebook was drafted by a model.
- **Not done, and said so:** the working name and any logo are not screened for trademarks; the identity is not tested with people; the look and feel step did not run; no logo image exists (`guidebook-not-held-1`, `business.guidebook.notYet.logoMark`). For that reason the guidebook is titled a draft (open question 7).
- **Simulated:** nothing in the product. Storybook stories run on labelled fixture projects.

## Story

As a founder or product owner who is signed in and has saved a business project, I want to open one guidebook of my brand from its board, with the brand foundation first and the visual identity after it, each part showing what it is, whether I gave it or a fixed rule worked it out and why, so that I see the brand as one document, a part that is still open says what it waits for and never shows a guess, a step I skipped is listed with everything that rests on it, and I know what this guidebook does not hold yet and why.

## Rules

### guidebook-assembly-1

One pure function of the engine, suggested `guidebookOf(view: BusinessView): Guidebook`, in `packages/intent-engine/src/business`. It reads only the view `toBusinessView` builds for the board of the same project, and fixed data of its own: the section members of `guidebook-sections-1`, the question keys of `guidebook-open-1`, the hypothesis labels of `guidebook-item-1` and the list of `guidebook-not-held-1`. It returns keys and values, never resolved text, so a language switch redraws with no second load. It imports no model module (`openrouter`, `llm-interpreter`), reads no clock, no environment and no random source, and stores nothing. It is run each time the page is loaded (open question 2). Because each item's state, value and marks are copied from the view's node, the guidebook and the board cannot disagree about a value, a state or a mark. The data says `method: "rule-based"`.

### guidebook-sections-1

The page's content, in this order:

1. The title (`business.guidebook.title`), the project name as typed, the saved date (`business.board.savedAt`), then `business.guidebook.byRule` and `business.guidebook.draftNote`, each once.
2. **The lead block** (`business.guidebook.section.business`, with `business.guidebook.businessNote`): `situation`, `business-core`, `audience`, `competitive-frame`, in that order, each by its title (`business.node.*`) and its board state line (`business.state.*` of the view's `nodes[].state`), and nothing else: no answer, no reading (open question 6).
3. **The foundation section** (`business.guidebook.section.foundation`): the items `positioning`, `identity-frame`, `architecture`, `name`, `core-idea`, in that order (open question 8).
4. **The visual section** (`business.guidebook.section.visual`): the items `logo-type`, `logo-mark`, `color`, `typography`, in that order; `logo-type`, `color` and `typography` read from ORV-298's view, which is in `develop` before PR 1 is built (open question 3).
5. **Not yet confirmed** (`business.guidebook.section.unconfirmed`), by `guidebook-unconfirmed-1`.
6. **Not in this guidebook** (`business.guidebook.section.notHeld`), by `guidebook-not-held-1`.
7. `business.guidebook.backToBoard`, a link to the project's board.

Both item orders are the ORV-126 fill order of group A and the order of node numbers (6, 7, 8, 9, 11; 13, 14, 15, 16).

### guidebook-item-1

What each item of the foundation and visual sections holds, for a done item (the board's result `given` or `derived`, or on `positioning` the gate `given` or `confirmed`; on `color`, also `open` with at least one decision made, ORV-298 `business.state.partly`). Every column reads the view; the labels are the ones the node's own honest rule places (ORV-291 `foundation-honest-1`, ORV-298 `expression-honest-1`, ORV-285 `positioning-honest-1`), shown as the board shows them.
