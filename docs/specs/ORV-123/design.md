# Sign-in notice: say "not done" once, keep the retry sentence whole

The `/account` sign-in notice keeps its ORV-112 shape (D2, D2a): a `danger` Badge word, then one line, in the `role="status"` region under the button, growing downward only. This design changes two things inside the line: the failure line gets its own copy that does not repeat the badge word (R1), and the limit line keeps its retry sentence on one line (R2). No component, token, recipe or layout value changes.

## 2. What the render shows today (the evidence behind the decisions)

Measured on `/account` at HEAD 98693f9 (scratch copy, signed out), the notice markup injected into the empty status region, every wait the sign-in limit can return (1 to 59 seconds, 1 to 10 minutes; window 600 s, `formatWait`):

| Width (content) | Thai today                                                                                            | English today                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 320 (288)       | "...ถี่เกินไป ลอง" / "ใหม่ได้ในอีก 7 นาที": the word "ลองใหม่" is cut in two                          | "...from this" / "network. Try again in 7 minutes." |
| 360 (328)       | "...ถี่เกินไป ลองใหม่ได้" / "ในอีก 7 นาที": the retry sentence is cut after "ได้"                     | "...this network." / "Try again in 7 minutes."      |
| 1280 (416)      | 1 line for 1 to 10 minutes; for 59 seconds "...ลองใหม่ได้ในอีก" / "59 วินาที": the wait itself is cut | "...Try again in" / "7 minutes.": the wait is cut   |

**F1, finding against the spec.** Spec item 5 and row 6 say the 360 Thai line breaks _inside_ "ในอีก 7 นาที". It does not: at 360 the phrase "ในอีก 7 นาที" already sits whole on line 2 (`today-limited-360-th-light.png`), which is what ORV-112 §13 S2 recorded. The flaw S2 names is the cut between "ลองใหม่ได้" and "ในอีก 7 นาที": the sentence "you can try again in 7 minutes" is split, so line 2 reads as a fragment. A row 6 that only checks "ในอีก 7 นาที" would pass today with no change. R2 therefore keeps the whole retry sentence "ลองใหม่ได้ในอีก {wait}" on one line, which contains "ในอีก 7 นาที" and so still meets row 6 as written. Suggested spec v1.1: row 6 and `orv123-wait-phrase` name "ลองใหม่ได้ในอีก 7 นาที" (the retry sentence) as the unit kept whole.

Today's failure notice in Thai reads "ทำไม่สำเร็จ" twice (`today-failed-360-th-light.png`). In English, "Not done" then "That did not work." says the same thing twice in different words.

Both rows keep D2a: the heading, paragraph and button do not move when the notice mounts (measured drift 0.004 to 0.005 px, budget 0.5), the button bottom to the status top is 12.00, and the page height stays 808 (no scroll) in every shot.

Thai read aloud: "ตอนนี้ยังลงชื่อเข้าใช้ไม่ได้ ลองใหม่อีกครั้ง" is how a Thai service says "you can't sign in just now, try again"; it states what did not work (sign-in) and the next step, without repeating "ทำไม่สำเร็จ", and its "ลองใหม่" matches the limit line's "ลองใหม่ได้ในอีก". It claims no cause (the failure may be a store outage or a missing secret), so it says neither "ขัดข้องชั่วคราว" nor "temporarily". English is changed with it so the two locales say the same thing (DESIGN.md §10, Thai defines the shape) and the English line no longer restates "Not done".

## 6. Render evidence

Rendered on `next dev` (port 3423) of a `git archive` of HEAD 98693f9 in the designer's scratchpad (`.../scratchpad/orv123/`), with throwaway auth settings and no `.env` read, so `/account` renders the real signed-out page (layout header, `main`, `SignInForm`, real `ds-button` and the app's CSS). Playwright Chromium 1.56.1, viewport width x 808, scale 2, reduced motion, focus on the button by keyboard. Scripts `scripts/lines.cjs` (line breaks per character) and `scripts/shoot.cjs`. The round "N" at the bottom left of every shot is the Next dev-mode indicator, not part of the page.

## 7. Observations outside this design

- Badge bottom to line top measures 10.44 px, not the 8 px of ORV-112 D2: the Badge sits in a plain inline `span` whose line box takes the body line-height. Unchanged by this story and visible in the ORV-122 sign-off as well; a later polish could make the wrapper `flex` so the gap is the region's `gap-2` alone.
