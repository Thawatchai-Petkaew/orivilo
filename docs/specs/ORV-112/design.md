# Rate-limit notices: sign-in, AI reading, writes, import, signed-out

A person who hits a limit is told in their language what did not happen and how long to wait. No raw error, no status code, no counter. This design places each notice where the person acted, composes it from components the catalog already has (Badge, Button, Card, the existing notice region of `SavedStudio`, the reading section of `DirectionStudio`), and adds no shared component. The catalog has no Alert entry; the house notice is a Badge word above a sentence in a polite live region (`SavedStudio`, ORV-35), and every notice here keeps that shape.

## 3. Flow

```
/account, signed out
  press "Sign in with Google" ──► pending: Button loading, repeat blocked
     ├─ allowed ──► redirect to Google (unchanged)
     └─ refused (sign-in, 10 per 10 min per network)
            ──► stay on /account; notice under the button: [Not done] + account.rateLimited with {wait}
            ──► button enabled again; pressing again replaces the notice with the new result

/directions, signed in (session may end while the page is open)
  Save draft / Approve ──► busy ──► refused (write or signed-out) ──► card notice: [Not done] + rate-limited {wait}
  Import a file        ──► busy ──► refused (import or signed-out) ──► card notice: [Not done] + rate-limited {wait}
                                   nothing saved, opened or replayed; every button enabled again
  Read with AI ──► "AI reading" (aria-busy) ──► refused (ai-read hour or day, or signed-out)
            ──► rules' reading stays, heading badge "Read by rules"
            ──► note under the button: [Check this] + ai-rate-limited {wait}   (or rate-limited {wait} when signed out)
            ──► "Read with AI" enabled again; editing the brief clears the note (existing rule)
```

Focus-visible is the Button's own ring in every state; no notice takes focus or moves it. Selected does not apply. Disabled does not apply to a notice; buttons are not disabled after a refusal (spec row 8).

## 7. The wait

- Formatted by the spec's rule (row 16): seconds under 60, else whole minutes rounded up, else whole hours rounded up, through `wait.seconds`, `wait.minutes`, `wait.hours`.
- Static: computed once from `retryAfter` when the outcome arrives. Not a countdown: a ticking number would churn the live region and DESIGN.md forbids a countdown without a reason to show one. Pressing again gets a fresh wait from the server.
- Missing data: if `retryAfter` is not a positive finite number, the line falls back to `errors.failed` ("That did not work. Try again." / "ทำไม่สำเร็จ ลองอีกครั้ง") rather than printing an empty or invalid wait.
- The day wait reaches hours ("14 hours", "14 ชั่วโมง"); both fit the same shape (checked below).

## 8. Light, dark, widths, Thai and English (from the mock)

- One start edge: in every surface the Badge's pill starts on the same edge as the button and the paragraph above it.
- Thai lines wrap to two or three lines at 360 without clipping; marks above and below (ทำไม่สำเร็จ, ชั่วโมง, ลองใหม่ได้) are whole in both modes. The Badge keeps Thai without tracking or caps.
- The AI note keeps one shape: word on its own line, sentence under it, at 360 and at 1280 (an inline badge beside the sentence was tried first and dropped: it changed shape between widths and sat 4 px under the button's focus ring).
- Dark mode: badges read on their dark tints; the line is primary ink. No colour is the only signal.
- Reduced motion: nothing new moves. The only motion is the Button's existing spinner.

## 10. Critique against the tells (DESIGN.md §9)

1 to 3, 5, 7, 10, 12: absent (no new surface, card, headline, numbered marker, preview change or per-page value). 4: no caps eyebrow, no middot, no em dash; the Badge words are sentence case. 6: no motion added. 8: lime is not used; warning and danger come from feedback tokens. 9: Thai set as Thai, no tracking. 11: no badge or count without data; the wait is the server's `retryAfter`, the AI label stays "Read by rules" because the rules read it.
