## Source intent

The owner, 2026-11-26, on the phone of brandbook page 11 (ORV-309 comment 10845), with the main session's reading: the phone shows the system's main function, not the homepage. The owner's crop of the built screen (the landing at 390) names three faults:

1. The top looks empty: under the status row the landing's header sits alone, then a tall gap before the title.
2. The first impression does not show what the app does: a headline and a paragraph about the product, the board only at the very bottom.
3. Text takes too much of the one screen: the title, a five-line paragraph and two buttons fill the screen.

Standing direction (ORV-309 comments 10829 and 10830, the owner's): digital first, the product is made of nodes, modern UX and UI, never a plain composition; a landing or an application-level example is composed from live components, never a generated picture; ORIVILO is the brand on show, so its lime intent button may appear once per page (R8, `intent-once-1`).

How the intent becomes design:

- Fault 2 and the direction make the board the subject. The phone shows ORIVILO's own board of connected nodes, the way the app shows it on a phone. Revision 3: four knowledge nodes (the brief, the audience, the personality, the feeling) feed the logo and the colour, and those two feed the brand guidebook. The edges are drawn in their port colours, and the graph reads as a real project that runs into one result.
- Fault 1, read on the whole board (revision 3, from the main session's reading of the revision 2 proof): no band of bare ground anywhere on the board. Every column holds work from under the bar down to the band above the button.
- Fault 1 puts an app bar right under the status row and the board right under the app bar. No band of empty ground anywhere above the first node.
- Fault 3 keeps every word a label the product itself draws: node names, one short line in three of the knowledge nodes, port names, "View only" and one button. No headline, no paragraph. Revision 3 shows the personality as three scales, not words, and drops two lines from revision 2: the brief's "Brand essence" and the guidebook card's "Brand guidelines", which repeated the label above the card.

### The edge rule (revision 2, held in revision 3), and how it is measured

The rule, held by the play of acceptance row A1b and read the same way by the next sign-off:

1. Every sample of every edge path lies inside the display (x 0 to 390, y 0 to 838.1) and inside the measure (x 16 to 374).
2. No sample lies under a card (`.ds-node` box), a label glyph (the rects of each node's `.ds-node__title` text), a shown port pill (`.ds-port__label` whose computed `visibility` is `visible`), the app bar (y under 102), the badge (`.ds-board__mode`) or the button, of any node, its own source and target included, except within the port's reach at its two ends: **12 design px** from the centre of its own source port or its own target port (the 16 px port glyph's half, 8, plus 4).
3. Every edge runs forward in the reading direction start to end: its target port's centre x is at or beyond its source port's centre x, and no sample steps back in x by more than 0.05 px from the one before.
4. Every sample keeps at least **6 design px** from the card, the title glyph rects, the port handles and the shown pills of each node that is neither its source nor its target.

Method (one method for the play and the sign-off): inside piece B's `[data-phone-screen]`, take its box `s` and `k = s.width / 390`. For each `path.ds-edge__path`, read the edge id from `.react-flow__edge[data-id]` (`<source>-<target>`), sample `getPointAtLength(L * i / 400)` for i 0 to 400 (401 samples) with `L = getTotalLength()`, map each point through `getScreenCTM()` to page px and then to design px as `((x - s.left) / k, (y - s.top) / k)`. Boxes are read with `getBoundingClientRect()` and mapped the same way; a port centre is the centre of its `.ds-port__handle` box (`data-handleid`); a title's glyph rects come from a `Range` over the `.ds-node__title` contents, clipped at the end of its `.ds-node__label` box. A box test is inclusive; a clearance is the distance from the sample to the nearest point of the box. Counts 1 to 3 must be 0; the least clearance of 4 must be 6 or more. Run it on page 3 of the In use preview in English and in Thai.
