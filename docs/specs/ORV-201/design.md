# ORV-201 design: Spinner and Skeleton (base)

The look of the two waiting states, the answers to open questions 1, 2 and 6, the `text` line height of `skel-shape-1`, and one row of CSS and markup per rule for the builder. Every value is an existing semantic token or one of the two component tokens the spec already names (`spinner.fg`, `skeleton.bg`); this design adds no token.

## Flow

1. A control the person pressed (Button) shows a `decorative` Spinner in its leading slot in the label's ink; the Button's own status speaks.
2. A small region (a row, a panel line) shows a Spinner `sm` or `md` with its label beside the ring.
3. A whole empty region (a card body, a panel) shows a Spinner `lg` with its label under the ring, centred.
4. A region whose layout is known shows a `SkeletonGroup` of static shapes in the places the content will take; one hidden status speaks; the caller swaps the group for the content.

## Answers to the open questions

| #   | Question                                      | Answer                                         | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Spinner motion under `prefers-reduced-motion` | (a), confirmed: the ring keeps turning         | Essential motion under SC 2.3.3 / C39 step 2 (K-0116) and SC 2.2.2 Note 4 (K-0117); the label carries the state, so motion is never the only carrier. The moving area is capped at 24 px (`size.icon.lg`), a small glyph with no travel and no scale, which is the low end of vestibular risk. The chat mark stops because it is a brand mark at rest, not a status glyph. Proven in `neutral-light-360-th-reduced.png`: computed `animationName` is `ds-spin` under emulated `reduce` |
| 2   | Skeleton pulse                                | (a), confirmed: static in every motion setting | DESIGN.md §3.3 (the engine at work is the only loop), SC 2.2.2 (K-0117), and no loop duration token. The static shapes read as placeholders on their own: one flat fill, one radius per shape, the rhythm of the content they stand for (screens below). Under reduced motion they are identical (computed `animationName` `none` in every shot)                                                                                                                                       |
| 6   | Label beside or under the ring                | Beside for `sm` and `md`, under for `lg`       | `sm` and `md` sit in a line of text or a row, so the pair is one inline unit; `lg` marks a whole empty region and stacks like the Table status (`.ds-table__status-inner`, a column). Gap beside: `space.inline.md` (8 px); gap under: `space.stack.sm` (8 px). Do not set an `lg` in one row with an `sm` or `md`: its label sits lower by design (see `neutral-light-1440-th.png`, top row)                                                                                          |

### Story composition for `Base/Skeleton` `Loading` and `At360`

The group inside a `Card`: a header row (`circle` 40 px, `size-10`, beside a `text` at 60 %, gap `space.inline.lg`), then two `text` lines at 100 % and 75 %, then a `block` at 100 % by 120 px. That is AC 13's one circle, three text lines and one block, and it is the shape of the matching content card in the screenshots (avatar, title, two `body.sm` lines, media).

## Spec amendments this design needs (L-0019)

1. AC 12, `circle`: replace "the resolved `--ds-shape-control-radius`" with "computed `clipPath` is `circle(50%)` and width equals height" (D11). The spec's radius makes a rounded square under professional (6 px).
2. AC 12, `text`: the radius check reads `getComputedStyle(el, "::before").borderTopLeftRadius` (D8, D9), and adds: host height equals `font-size * line-height` of `type.body.sm` to 0.5 px.
3. `spin-colour-1` and the contract table: the `spinner.fg` pairs are listed at floor 4.5, not 3.0 (D7).
4. `skel-shape-1` last paragraph: the `text` line height is `type.body.sm` (no new token).
