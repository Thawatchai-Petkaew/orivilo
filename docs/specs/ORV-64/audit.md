# ORV-64 audit: component coverage and depth

Date: 2026-10-23. Research only. No code, catalog or `coverage.md` change.

Goal from the owner: cover what shadcn/ui and Untitled UI cover, with variant depth comparable to Untitled UI, plus ORIVILO's own components, plus an AI elements set.

How to read this file:

- "Ours" comes from `packages/design-system/catalog/{base,application}/*.yaml`, `catalog/coverage.md` and the props in `src/components/*`. Toast, Progress and Tag are being built now, so they count as in progress, not as done.
- shadcn names are the index at https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components as the fetch tool returned it (66 names). Several names in it (Bubble, Marker, Message Scroller, Questionnaire, Trace, Attachment, Direction, Item) are not in my training data; I did not open their pages. They are listed as found and marked "page not read".
- Untitled UI names come from https://www.untitledui.com/react/components (the category list the fetch tool returned) plus the component pages I read. Variant facts in section B come from those pages only.
- "not checked" means I did not read that page. I did not guess.
- Colour rules applied (DESIGN.md section 8 and AGENTS.md): status colour comes only from feedback tokens; brand accent never replaces error, warning, selection or focus; no decorative colour variant without meaning; every string in Thai and English; no middot and no em dash.
