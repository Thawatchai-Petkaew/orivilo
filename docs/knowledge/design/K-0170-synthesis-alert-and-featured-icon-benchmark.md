### What the standards say

- Role alert: assertive and atomic; focus need not move; no forced close; `alertdialog` for moving focus or taking a response (K-0162). The APG agrees on focus and adds that an alert should not disappear on its own (K-0163). Its timing and interruption cautions name 2.2.3 and 2.2.4, both Level AAA (K-0163).
- Status message: SC 4.1.3 is Level AA (K-0115). The page lists role status for results and state, and role alert or live regions for suggestions and errors (K-0164). Role status is polite and atomic (K-0114, K-0166). An error in a dialog that takes focus is out of its scope (K-0164).
- Colour alone: SC 1.4.1 is Level A and needs a visible alternative; it does not cover assistive technology, which other criteria do (K-0167). F81 names required and error fields (K-0168). SC 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for graphics required for understanding; its status icon example says "without associated text", and no sentence exempts an icon paired with text (K-0169).

### Statements scoped to the pages read

- Live behaviour: of the two libraries, only shadcn shows it, and only in source (K-0160). Untitled's is unknown, not absent (K-0158).
- Dismiss: Untitled's is optional and shadcn has none (K-0158, K-0160). Neither page read requires a close.
- Container first: both technique tests put the role on the container before the message, and ARIA19 says the container "must be present" on page load (K-0165, K-0166). That is a condition of the technique, which is one sufficient way; neither page read names a node mounted with its text as a failure.
- Every `role="alert"` hit in `packages/design-system/src/components/**/*.tsx` (pattern `role="alert"`, 5 hits) sits inside a condition on the error, so it mounts with its message.

### Open points

- Mount order. The five repo nodes mount with their text, and K-0165 and K-0166 test a role already in place. No source read shows whether the repo's order announces; a screen reader test would settle it.
- Alert at load. A failed job can already be on the page when a user arrives, and K-0163 says such an alert is not spoken. That wording says "at this time" and carries no test.
- Which role per kind. Success sits in Situation A and danger in Situation B on our reading; info and warning depend on the message (K-0164).
- Alert or Toast. `toast.yaml` line 8 uses Toast for "generation failed", and D.3 row 19 of `audit.md` gives Alert "Inline errors for failed jobs". `DESIGN.md` section 6 has no row between them.
- Icons. `icons.tsx` has one warning glyph and no info glyph, and `DESIGN.md` line 187 puts Lucide first. Which glyph each kind takes is the designer's call.
- FeaturedIcon outside feedback. `audit.md` line 74 names Empty state as a use, and D.3 row 19 says four feedback kinds only, so a neutral tile has no token decision yet.
- `feedback.*.border` is listed as decorative (`contrast-pairs.ts` line 162), so an alert edge in that token cannot be what tells a user the kind.
