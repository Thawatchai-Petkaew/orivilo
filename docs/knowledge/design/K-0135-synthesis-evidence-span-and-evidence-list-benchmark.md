### Statements scoped to the pages read

- None of the AI Elements pages or files read (K-0127, K-0128) sets an `aria-*` attribute beyond the carousel's Previous and Next labels; ARIA comes from the primitives they wrap.
- Of the primitives read for K-0127 to K-0134, only Radix Hover Card takes the tabbables of its open content out of the tab order (K-0129); a closed Radix Collapsible renders no children, which is not that case (K-0126); Radix Popover passes focus handling to `FocusScope` and returns focus to the trigger unless the user interacted outside (K-0130).
- No standard page read names citations, footnotes or source lists (K-0132, K-0133, K-0134). Applying Button, Disclosure and 1.4.13 to them is our reading.
- The Ours rows record only the files listed in `source:`; other components were found only through the greps named above.

### Open points

- Whether EvidenceSpan opens by press only, or also on hover with the 1.4.13 conditions, is for the designer (K-0134).
- Whether the card is modal is open; Radix defaults to non-modal (K-0130) and the APG only describes the modal dialog (K-0133).
- Where Radix `FocusScope` puts focus when a non-modal popover opens was not read (K-0130); our `Popover.tsx` comment says Radix moves focus inside.
- Untitled UI's repo was not searched for a quote component for this question (K-0131).
