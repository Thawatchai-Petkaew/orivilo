### GICS, from MSCI's GICS page (read directly)

> The four tiers are: Sectors, Industry Groups, Industries and Sub-Industries.

> Each company is assigned a single GICS® classification in each of the four tiers, according to its principal business activity.

> Revenues is a key factor in determining a firm's principal business activity.

The page states no counts and no license terms beyond "All rights reserved." and a terms-of-use link.

### GICS counts and the consumer sectors, from Wikipedia (read directly)

> 11 sectors, 25 industry groups, 74 industries and 163 sub-industries

Wikipedia states "GICS" is a registered trademark of McGraw Hill Financial and MSCI Inc. The two consumer sectors at industry level:

- 25 Consumer Discretionary: 251010 Automobile Components, 251020 Automobiles, 252010 Household Durables, 252020 Leisure Products, 252030 Textiles, Apparel & Luxury Goods, 253010 Hotels, Restaurants & Leisure, 253020 Diversified Consumer Services, 255010 Distributors, 255030 Broadline Retail, 255040 Specialty Retail (10 industries).
- 30 Consumer Staples: 301010 Consumer Staples Distribution & Retail, 302010 Beverages, 302020 Food Products, 302030 Tobacco, 303010 Household Products, 303020 Personal Care Products (6 industries).

### GS1 Global Product Classification (below 0.7)

gs1.org pages returned 403. Search excerpts of GS1 Netherlands material ("GPC in a nutshell", June 2024) describe GPC as GS1's scheme sorting products into four levels (segment, family, class, brick), with the 8-digit brick as the working level, updated twice a year (May and November). A GS1 article title reports 1,636 bricks; its date was not visible, so the current count is unknown. GPC classifies trade items, so a company or a brand has no single GPC code.

### What we infer (below 0.7)

- GICS has no public Thai data keyed to it at the scale of DBD registrations; Thai listed companies are classed by SET's own industry groups, not GICS (a SET document on its industry grouping appeared in search but was not read). So GICS cannot rank Thai new-brand formation without a mapping.
- GICS is shaped for listed companies: small consumer services that found many brands (cafes, salons, clinics, tutoring) fall into a few broad sub-industries such as Hotels, Restaurants & Leisure or Diversified Consumer Services.
- GPC fits product categories on a shelf, not service brands, and has no Thai formation data attached.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

ORV-127 weighs GICS and a consumer scheme against ISIC and TSIC. GICS is the investor view with a known small count (163 at the finest level), but it is trademarked and has no Thai registration data keyed to it; GPC covers products only. Which scheme to use is the architect's decision.
