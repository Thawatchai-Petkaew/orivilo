## Evidence

Bibliographic details are consistent across several reference lists. No exact quote is given here, because only search summaries were read, not the abstract at the publisher. In our words, the summaries say the paper develops guidelines for choosing typefaces that create strategically valued impressions, simplifies the choice to six underlying design dimensions, and discusses trade-offs between impressions. One secondary source names the six dimensions as elaborate, harmony, natural, flourish, weight and compressed.

Confidence 0.6: the article itself, its samples and its typefaces were not read; the dimension names come from one secondary summary. Use as a lead only.

### What the sources do not show

- Which design features drive which impression, with effect sizes.
- Whether the findings, measured on Latin typefaces with Western respondents, hold for Thai script or Thai readers.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

It is the main academic lead for "typography carries personality", which `brand-ci-method.md` §3 relies on in its personality-to-type mapping. Before any rule cites it, someone should read the article and raise or lower this entry.
