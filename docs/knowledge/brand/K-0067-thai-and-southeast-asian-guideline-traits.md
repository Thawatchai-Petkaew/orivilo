## Evidence

> Thainess is expressed through the discipline of the work.

(ThaiGraph, "Best Thai Brand Identity Work", an editorial opinion with no cited evidence, as returned by the fetch)

Confidence is 0.6: two of the six sources were read directly (art4d, TAT), the Mahidol manual, Grab and Gojek only through search summaries, and ThaiGraph is opinion.

## What was seen

- Thai script as its own design task. Farmgroup rearranged the Thai lettering of the city name and drew a Thai display typeface from a royal figure's handwriting for the Bangkok identity (art4d, read). TikTok's book has a localization page for accented scripts, but no Thai rule (see [[K-0060-tiktok-for-business-guidelines-sections]]).
- Rules for official documents. The Mahidol faculty CI manual (via search summary) sets fonts for official letters apart from fonts for design work, and covers letterhead, business cards, website, slides and event backdrops.
- Cultural meaning as the reason. The Amazing Thailand logo reshapes the Kanok pattern into a smile and the "A" into smiling eyes (TAT, read); the Bangkok green refers to Indra (art4d, read). See [[K-0065-how-guidelines-show-each-elements-reason]].
- An official file source. The Bangkok system is meant as the reference for where to get the official files, because departments had been using different emblem versions (art4d, read).
- Partner-facing centres. Grab publishes logo, color and naming rules for merchants per sub-brand (via search summary).
- Large regional books. Gojek's book runs 200+ pages and names sonic branding (via search summary).

## Opinion recorded, not adopted

ThaiGraph argues that strong Thai identity work signals culture through restraint, proportion and typography rather than temple silhouettes, traditional patterns or gold-and-red color. A Monocle piece (via search summary) says Thai graphic design still lacks a clearly defined national identity. Neither cites evidence.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

ORIVILO's first customers are Thai. A guidebook for them likely needs a Thai and English type pairing, an official-document page, and a place to state cultural meaning without falling into the cliches the ThaiGraph piece names. This is a lead for the designer to check, not a rule.
