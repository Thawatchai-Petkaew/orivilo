> Our visual language is an extension of the logo.

(page 10, "Design Inspiration")

> Our colors take advantage of the existing TikTok brand colors that hold brand equity. They are designed to reflect the youthful and contemporary energy of the TikTok brand.

(page 35, "Color Palette")

Both quotes were found again on 2026-10-26 in the public SlideShare copy, on slides 10 and 35 (see [[K-0060-tiktok-for-business-guidelines-sections]] for that copy). Its text puts the leading rule ("By setting the leading, or line height, to 120% of the font size, our system ensures that text does not collide.") on slide 32 and the accent rule ("Do not use Splash (#25F4EE) to highlight text, as it does not meet accessibility standards.") on slide 31, so the page 31 below may be one page early for the leading rule. It shows "DOWNLOAD" labels on slides 12, 22, 26, 34 and 38.

## Layout, in our words

- Format: landscape slides at 16:9, one topic per page, page number bottom right, brand lockup bottom left.
- Breadcrumb header on every page: document name, section, subsection (for example "Design System / Logo / Clearspace").
- Section dividers: a full black page with the section number in red, the section name very large, and the list of its subsections. A reader can navigate by dividers alone.
- Rule pages: a light grey left column (about a third of the width) holds a red eyebrow label, the rule title, one short paragraph, then "Things To Do" and "Things To Avoid" bullet lists. The right two thirds hold the specimen, mockup or photos with small captions.
- Misuse pages: the rule is a numbered list on the left; the right shows the same numbers as drawn examples crossed with a red X and a one-line caption. Do and Don't pairs sit side by side with the labels "Do" and "Don't" under each image.
- Values are printed on the page: HEX, Pantone, RGB and CMYK under each color swatch; size, weight, tracking, leading and minimum size per type level.
- Download buttons sit on the pages for the logo animation and the color palette, so the book points to the files rather than holding them.

## How reasons are shown, in our words

- A derivation page: page 10 draws the logo, then its two circles, then the rounded bars and marks made from them, joined by arrows. One page explains the origin of the whole toolkit.
- A purpose line per rule: the horizontal lockup is "primary", the vertical one for tight or vertical formats; text leading is 120% so accents in other languages do not collide (page 31); one accent color must not highlight text because it fails accessibility (pages 31, 36).
- Personality as the reason for tone: the personality page states the character, and the tone page turns it into Do and Don't words and sentences.
- Reasons are short and local to the rule. There is no separate "why" chapter beyond the rationale page.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

ORIVILO generates the guidebook, so it can reproduce this grid as a template: one element per page, rule plus reason on the left, evidence on the right, misuse numbered and crossed out. It also shows that a reason can be one sentence tied to the rule, which suits a trace from each element to its source reason.
