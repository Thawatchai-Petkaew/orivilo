## Evidence

> Our design system is composed of four core elements: logo, typography, color, and a design toolkit with specific shapes, patterns, and graphic symbols.

(page 11, "Design System Overview")

## What the document holds, in our words

Read from 44 of the 70 rendered pages (1 to 11, 13, 17, 22, 24, 25, 27, 30 to 33, 35 to 37, 39, 44, 47 to 52, 54 to 56, 58, 62 to 64, 66 to 70). The rest were not opened; sound or sonic content was not seen on any page read.

Two tables of contents exist. Page 2 lists four sections (Messaging, Design System, Imagery, Advertising Guidelines). Page 62 lists five, adding "01 Brand Story" in front. Pages 63 to 69 carry that brand story and are stamped "WIP". The document is a living draft, not a finished book.

1. Brand story (pages 63 to 69, WIP): mission, TikTok For Business mission, vision, brand promise, audiences (small businesses, enterprises, agencies, priority verticals), the platform line "Don't Make Ads. Make TikToks.", and a global portfolio with one insight, one promise and one creative adaptation line per sister app.
2. Messaging (pages 3 to 8): platform statement, personality (one headline sentence and four short paragraphs, including how much personality to show at each funnel stage), tone of voice as a Do and Don't word list, then the same as Do and Don't example sentences, and brand name treatment in copy (full name, capital F, abbreviations internal only).
3. Design system (pages 9 to 46):
   - Rationale and inspiration: one page that derives every toolkit shape from the two circles of the logo, drawn as a chain of arrows.
   - Overview: one page that shows the four core elements side by side.
   - Logo: lockups (horizontal primary, vertical secondary, with when to use each), social lockups, color variations, scale, clearspace (equal to the cap height of the "T"), partnerships, placement, rotation, logo animation, application mockups (business card, website, newsletter, social, banners), and six numbered things to avoid, each drawn and crossed out in red.
   - Typography: brand typefaces with specimen, substitutes, a type system (H1 4.5x, H2 3x, subhead 1.5x, body 1x, caption 0.5x, with weight, tracking, leading, alignment and minimum sizes), headline highlight treatment, localization (leading at 120% so accents do not collide), and fifteen numbered things to avoid.
   - Color: four named core colors with HEX, Pantone, RGB and CMYK, an extended palette, color rules (accessibility, overload, focus) shown as Do and Don't pairs, and four things to avoid.
   - Toolkit: rounded rectangles, bubbles, patterns, pattern application, emphasis marks, each with things to do and to avoid.
4. Imagery (pages 47 to 54, 70): photography guidelines (subject matter, "Style and Properties", "Content" lists, four captioned example groups), diversity (casting by race, culture, age, gender), photography application (image masks with 25% or 50% corner roundness, rotation in 10 degree steps, colored borders), six photo things to avoid, video rules (energy, authenticity, vertical 9:16), video Do and Don't, and illustration rules.
5. Advertising guidelines (pages 55 to 61): video (intro, middle, outro), stills, graphics, banners, Do and Don't.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

This is the owner's chosen reference for the CI guidebook (ORV-126). It gives a concrete section list, the depth of rules per element (values, sizes, misuse pages), and shows that a strong guidebook opens with story and voice before any visual rule.
