## Evidence

Each URL above was fetched in this task on 2026-10-26 and read as untrusted page text; quotes are as the fetch returned them. Behance projects come from [[K-0070-mood-carried-by-applications-in-context]] and [[K-0062-brand-guideline-projects-studied]], read in a browser earlier the same day, and are not re-fetched (Behance returns 403).

How a count works: "named on N" means N of the pages read for that section name the touchpoint in text or in a caption the fetch returned. A page that does not name a touchpoint may still show it in an image. Most pages are agency or vendor case studies, so they show what the studio chose to present, not what the business uses most. The section of each business is our reading of its activity against the TSIC 2009 section names ([[K-0048-tsic-2009-is-isic-rev4-based-at-five-digits]]), not a code the business states. Confidence is 0.7 and not higher: two to four pages per section, chosen by search, not random, and section B rests on weak pages.

Pages that were tried and gave nothing usable, so not counted: FLS (an equipment supplier, page names no touchpoints), orbit.scot Scotgold (404), So Energy on designweek.co.uk (403), xamax.co.uk (403), contra.com portfolio pages (empty or 404), the Bailey's Farms page on thedieline.com (names products, not applications).

## A. Agriculture, forestry and fishing (three pages)

- Millbank Farm, Northern Ireland, by Jack Renwick Studio (identitydesigned.com): "farm signage, clothing, business cards, pallet boxes, a website, product stickers, recipe cards, paper bags".
- Rostellan Farm, Irish family dairy (100archive.com): "The outputs included brand identity, packaging, signage, print and digital advertising."
- Philo Ridge Farm, Vermont (gdusa.com): "a retail signage system including custom updatable menu boards, shelf displays and informational signs", plus packaging.
- Named on: signage 3; packaging, labels, stickers or bags 3; website 1; work clothing 1; business card 1; advertising 1; menu board or shelf display 1.
- Thin spot: all three farms sell direct to the public and two process their own produce (dairy is section C, class 1050). No commodity grower (rice, rubber, fishing fleet) was read.

## B. Mining and quarrying (thin evidence, three weak pages)

- A design blog on mining and steel marks (madegooddesigns.com), about the BHP name, describes where a heavy-industry mark goes in general, not BHP's own rules: "It is printed on hard hats and high-visibility clothing, stencilled on ore wagons and locomotives," and names gatehouse signs, ship hulls and haul truck bodies.
- Maaden, Saudi mining company (campaignme.com): a brand campaign on digital platforms and out-of-home advertising, and the wordmark on a sponsored Formula One car: "Maaden's branding features prominently on the car's livery."
- Mawsons, Australian quarries and concrete (mawsons.com.au): after a merger, "other Milbrae branding on equipment, corporate documents, promotional materials and site signage" moves to the Mawsons logos and livery.
- Named on: site signage 2; vehicles or equipment 2; PPE (hard hats, high-visibility clothing) 1; corporate documents 1; advertising 1; sponsorship livery 1.
- Thin spot: no mining company's own guideline or a studio case study for one was read. The strongest list is a third-party blog's general description.

## C. Manufacturing (three pages, plus Yumy from K-0070)

- Huskie Tools, utility tool maker (casestudies.com, Streng Agency): the identity "carried the new identity through all content, including trade show brochures, catalogs, and web updates", with new product photography and the product line itself.
- Cortegra, pharmaceutical label printer (turchette.com): "Booth graphics, public relations, a new website and supporting collateral helped to complete the re-branding of the company.", with trade press advertising.
- Frooti, mango drink, by Pentagram (pentagram.com): the packaging range, "from litre bottles to 90ml pouches".
- Yumy, Vietnamese food brand (K-0070, Behance): the logo on product photographs.
- Named on: packaging or the product 3 (Frooti, Yumy, Huskie's product line); website 2; trade show booth or brochure 2; catalogue 1; trade advertising 1.
- Thin spot: section C runs from food to steel to electronics (divisions 10 to 33). The two consumer brands show packaging; the two industrial makers show booth, catalogue and website. One set will fit one half badly.

## D. Electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply (two pages, plus Evolta and Leadeep from K-0070)

- Insite Energy, UK heat networks with metering and billing (insite-energy.co.uk): "You can expect to see these bright designs on our vans and staff uniforms when we’re on site."
- AES Solar, UK solar and heat pumps (aessolar.co.uk): new workwear first, so teams are "safe and identifiable" on site, then van livery.
- Evolta, solar energy (K-0070): polo shirt, billboard, business cards, letterhead, hard hat, branded tape, building sign, app store listing, phone home-screen icon, lanyard, icon set.
- Leadeep, solar (K-0070, contents page): stationery, social media covers, merchandise.
- Named on: work clothing 3; service vans 2; stationery 2; app 1; billboard 1; building sign 1; social media 1.
- Thin spot: AES Solar and probably Evolta install systems, which may be section F (class 4321, electrical installation) rather than D. No utility or power generator's own guideline was read.

## E. Water supply; sewerage, waste management and remediation (two pages)

- Tidy, Singapore waste management (designsingapore.org): "their building, gates, trucks and bins have since been given a spanking new look", and "staff uniforms to stand out".
- Renewi, Netherlands and UK waste (vim-group.com): the rebrand ran "across its various brand carriers (locations, vehicles, work clothing and containers)".
- Named on: trucks 2; bins or containers 2; work clothing 2; buildings, gates or locations 2.
- The two pages agree on all four. Thin spot: no water utility was read.

## F. Construction (two pages)

- TruNorth Construction (fastsigns.com, a sign maker's case study): "design vehicle wraps for every vehicle in the fleet", "lawn signs, H-Frames, and temporary posts and panels at every job site", and equipment and hard hats, office wall graphics, client presentation boards.
- Flynn, Irish fit-out and construction (100archive.com): "A contrasting colour palette works across every facet of the brands reach from livery, tenders to hoarding and cranes.", plus a new website.
- Named on: vehicle livery 2; site signs or hoarding 2; hard hats, equipment or cranes 2; tender or presentation documents 2; website 1; office wall graphics 1.
- Thin spot: TruNorth's page is written by a sign maker, so it leans to signs.
