## Evidence

Umbrex lists the components under "The Components of the Brand Key":

> "Competitive Environment (Frame of Reference)" "Target (Who We Serve)" "Consumer Insight (Unlocking Truth)"
> "Benefits (Functional and Emotional)" "Reasons to Believe (RTBs)" "Values & Personality (Character)"
> "Discriminator (Point of Difference)" "Root Strength (Heritage/Core Competence)" "Brand Essence (The Key)"

It says the Key "typically comprises nine interlocking components, culminating in a central Brand Essence" and was "Developed within Unilever's brand management practice". A template found by search lists eight boxes around the essence, in a different order.

Confidence 0.65: two secondary sources agree on the parts, but no Unilever document was found, the order differs between them, and the usage durations on the Umbrex page are the consultancy's.

### What the sources do not show

- An official Unilever description, date or version.
- Any link from the Key to visual identity beyond values and personality.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

The Key is a lead, not a ground for a decision: it is a compact input checklist for the strategy side (target, insight, frame of reference, discriminator, essence) that a brief form could mirror. Below 0.7, it may not be cited as the reason for a rule.
