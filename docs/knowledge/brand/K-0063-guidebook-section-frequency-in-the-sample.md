## Evidence

Our own counts over the projects in [[K-0062-brand-guideline-projects-studied]]. No published study that counts guideline sections was found; a search for one returned practitioner guides only.

Revised on 2026-10-26: the first version (confidence 0.55) rested on eleven projects, six of them known only through search summaries. A second sample of 18 Behance projects was then read in a real browser (screenshot on file, see K-0062), and its counts agree with the first on which sections are common and which are rare. Confidence is raised to 0.7 for that agreement, not higher: both samples are small and not random, the second is sorted by appreciations and crowded with templates, and in it only one or two tiles per project were read (all five for Hedwig), plus the contents pages of Leadeep, Aspot, Olade and Atma. A count is "seen in N"; a project not counted may still hold the section.

## Sample 1, eleven projects (as first written)

The eleven, numbered as in K-0062: TikTok (1), Gojek Brand Book (2), Grab brand centre (4), FIFTHSIP (7), 82-page kit (10), Uber (13), DocuSign (14), BMA by Farmgroup (17), Amazing Thailand (18), Mahidol CI manual (19), Banyan Tree Veya (20).

What most guidelines include (the baseline):

- Logo, confirmed in 10 (all but the 82-page kit). Variants or lockups, clear space, minimum size, color versions, misuse examples.
- Typography, confirmed in 8 (1, 2, 7, 13, 14, 17, 19, 20). Typefaces, levels, substitutes. Thai sources add separate fonts for official letters and for design (19) or Thai lettering plus a custom display face (17).
- Color, confirmed in 7 (1, 2, 4, 13, 14, 17, 18). Named primaries with HEX, RGB, CMYK and Pantone values; secondary or extended palettes.
- Graphic elements (patterns, shapes, supergraphics, icons), confirmed in 7 (1, 2, 13, 14, 17, 19, 20).
- Imagery (photography, illustration or both), confirmed in 6 (1, 2, 10, 13, 14, 17).
- Applications or mockups, confirmed in 6 (1, 7, 14, 17, 19, 20). Business card, letterhead, website, social, signage, slides, merchandise.
- Story, concept or meaning, confirmed in 5 (1, 7, 17, 18, 20). Often one concept line: a smile from the Kanok pattern (18), a Mobius strip for lifelong wellbeing (20), green from Indra's body (17).
- Tone of voice as a section, confirmed in 4 (1, 2, 10, 13).

What only the larger or stronger books add:

- Motion and logo animation, 3 (1, 13, 14), all global brands.
- Sound or a sonic logo, 2 (2, 14).
- A derivation or rationale page that explains where the system comes from, 3 (1, 17, 20).
- Downloads or an official file source linked from the guide, 3 (1, 14, 17).
- Layout or composition grid, 2 (2, 13).
- Inclusion and casting rules for photography, 2 (1, 13).
- Localization or multi-script rules, 2 (1, 17).
- Channel rules (ads, video, product UI), 2 (1, 14).

## Sample 2, 18 Behance projects read in a browser (fact, on the tiles read)

The 18: Hedwig, Evolta, Truway, Jorixent (the owner's four), Leadeep, Aspot, Olade, Kryon, Atma, Mose, Green Lead, Sit, Discord, Khaolak Home Thong, The Mangrove Panwa Phuket, True Money Wallet, Artkai startup cases, Yumy.

- Logo, seen in 18.
- Overview, story, concept or motto text, seen in 15 (all but Green Lead, Khaolak and Yumy on the tiles read).
- Applications or mockups, seen in 13 (Hedwig, Evolta, Truway, Jorixent, Khaolak, Mangrove, Discord, True Money, Artkai, Yumy; listed in the contents of Leadeep, Aspot, Olade).
- Colour, seen in 9 (Hedwig, Evolta, Truway, Jorixent, Leadeep, Aspot, Olade, Atma, Yumy).
- Typography, seen in 9 (Hedwig, Truway, Jorixent, Leadeep, Aspot, Olade, Atma, Green Lead, Yumy).
- Clear space or minimum size, seen in 9 (Truway, Leadeep, Aspot, Olade, Kryon, Mose, Green Lead, Sit, Yumy).
- Patterns or icon sets, seen in 7 (Hedwig, Evolta, Leadeep, Aspot, Green Lead, Discord, Mangrove).
- A construction grid for the mark, seen in 6 (Hedwig, Evolta, Jorixent, Leadeep, Kryon, Mose).
- The logo's reason stated, seen in 6 (see [[K-0069-logo-reason-as-an-equation-of-parts]]).
- Misuse examples, seen in 4 (Leadeep, Olade, Green Lead, Yumy).
- Tone of voice, 2 (Kryon, Aspot). Illustration as a system, 2 (Hedwig, Discord). Product screens as part of the identity, 2 (Hedwig, Discord).
- Motion, 1 (Discord). A personality slider of six opposite pairs, 1 (Kryon).
- Sound, 0. A mood-board page, 0 (see [[K-0070-mood-carried-by-applications-in-context]]).

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

A guidebook spec could treat logo, colour, typography, a short overview with the reason for each element, and applications as the minimum an ORIVILO guidebook holds, and the rarer sections as what makes one feel complete. At 0.7 this may ground a decision on the minimum set; the exact counts are still a sample, not a rate.
