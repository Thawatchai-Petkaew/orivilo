## What each page shows (fact, seen on the tiles)

- Hedwig, a digital library for reading and listening, by Roman Lieli (Barcelona). Two opening cards (what the service is, and the goal: a corporate identity plus the mobile app and the web version). Then app screens and a person listening, the owl mark, three colour swatches (yellow, light grey, near black) and three type weights (Regular, Medium, Bold), a construction grid, the wordmark, brand book pages (cover, illustrations, logo, icons), more app screens, a billboard with the app on a phone, a laptop, a full web landing page, an icon set and a set of spot illustrations. 631 appreciations, 9.7K views, 245 comments, published 29 September 2026. The role names on the swatches were too small to read; "accent, surface, text" rests on the orchestrator's note.
- Evolta, solar energy branding. A "Project Overview" text (what the company does, the objective, the result, the services: logo, colour, typeface), a hero photo, an applications grid, the logo on a construction grid, a sheet of eight symbol variants with the chosen one circled, the equation "Letter E + Sun + Home = Evolta Solar", then an icon set and applications.
- Truway, a travel agency. A hero photo of a coastal road, a two-paragraph introduction, the equation "Letter T + Pathway + Arrow Icon + Tree Icon = Brand Mark", a clear-space frame, four named colours with values (Pure White, Carolina Blue, Flickering Sea, Midnight Blue), the typeface (Noto Sans, four weights), a mission paragraph, then travel photography, tagline cards and stationery.
- Jorixent, a 3D architectural visualisation studio, by Obrazur Brands. A hero portrait with the logo, one paragraph that explains the mark (the letters J and r built from quarter ellipses), the palette (red orange, white, grey, black, chosen to set the studio apart from competitors) and the typeface (Neue Montreal), then construction grids, the wordmark on a grid, numbered poster pages and applications.

## What makes Hedwig the closest fit (inference, 0.6)

ORIVILO's line is "from business intent to living digital products" (AGENTS.md). Of the four, Hedwig is the only page whose stated goal joins the identity and the product, and whose sequence ends in product screens and a website built from the same yellow, type and illustrations; the other three end at print, signage and merchandise mockups. The visitor's comment above names the same quality: one visual system holding two product modes. Whether this is the reason the owner gave is not recorded; the owner should confirm.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

The reference set shows the shape the owner likes: a short reasoned overview, a compact rule set, then the identity proven in use. Hedwig adds the step ORIVILO exists for, the product itself, which a Phase 1 guidebook could point toward even if it does not build it.
