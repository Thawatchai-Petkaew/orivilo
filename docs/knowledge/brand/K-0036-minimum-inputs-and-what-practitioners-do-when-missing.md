## Evidence

This entry compares what the entries above record; each quote is sourced there. The comparison is ours.

Per method, the inputs it needs before visual design, then what it does when they are missing:

- **Wheeler (K-0028).** Needs vision, strategy, goals and values; stakeholder needs; market, competitive, legal and language audits; positioning; a brand brief. When missing, Phase 1 produces them: "Interview key management.", the audits, "Present audit readout."
- **Neumeier (K-0030).** Needs who you are, what you do, why it matters; vision; competitors; onliness. When missing, checkpoints 1 to 6 are worked through in order, and prototypes and tests validate.
- **Kapferer prism (K-0031).** Needs the six facets from customer and internal evidence. When missing, the consultancy summary drafts a current-state prism from evidence, then a target state.
- **Aaker (K-0032).** Needs a core identity and the product, organization, person and symbol perspectives. What to do when missing is not shown in the source read.
- **Brand Key (K-0034, 0.65).** Needs frame of reference, target, insight, benefits, reasons to believe, discriminator, essence. The summary says insight comes from interviews, ethnography, reviews and social listening.
- **Design102 (K-0029).** For an existing brand, needs the equity of current elements. It starts with "Conducting an audit at the beginning of the project."
- **Wolff Olins (K-0035).** Needs a theme or core idea. It runs workshops (Tate) or immersion on site and staff conversations (NYBG).

Confidence 0.75: the pattern holds across every method read and rests on entries at 0.7 or above, except the Brand Key row (0.65), which is shown for completeness and is not needed for the claim.

### What the sources do not show

- A published minimum below which a practitioner refuses to start, or how many questions a brief must answer.
- Any method that fills missing strategy with neutral defaults. The absence is in the sources read; it does not prove nobody does it, and fast, low-budget practice (templates, logo makers) was not surveyed.
- How long research takes when inputs are missing; the durations seen are consultancies' own estimates.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

`brand-ci-method.md` §2 fills some missing inputs with defaults (for example 50 on every personality axis). The practice read here instead treats a missing strategic input as work to do (ask, research, run a short workshop) before design. Which way ORIVILO goes is for the designer and the owner.
