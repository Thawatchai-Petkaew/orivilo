## Evidence

> The 24 lines in the original symbol inspired the stripe details

(art4d on Farmgroup's Bangkok Metropolitan Administration identity, 19 December 2023, as returned by the fetch)

## The five ways, each with where it was seen (fact)

1. Derived from another element. TikTok page 10 draws the chain from the logo's two circles to bubbles, bars, patterns and emphasis marks. The BMA stripe pattern comes from the 24 lines of the old emblem. Uber's grid is built from the logo (via search summary, see [[K-0062-brand-guideline-projects-studied]]).
2. A cultural or brand meaning. Amazing Thailand: the smile symbol reshapes the Kanok pattern, the "A" reads as smiling eyes, the colors were chosen for softness. BMA: the emerald green refers to the color of Indra's body; secondary colors come from Bangkok street objects to stand for diversity.
3. A functional need. BMA made short "BMA" and "BKK" logotypes because the emblem's detail fails at small sizes. TikTok sets leading at 120% so accents do not collide, and bars one accent color from highlighting text because it fails accessibility. TikTok names a primary and a secondary lockup by the formats each serves.
4. A problem in the old identity. BMA had no original emblem file, so departments used different versions; the redraw and official file source answer that. Gojek (via search summary) moved to six colors because it had run out of distinct colors for new products.
5. A link to a brand value or promise. TikTok's colors "reflect the youthful and contemporary energy" of the brand (page 35). FIFTHSIP's serif letterforms carry the elegance and premium quality the brand promises (Behance editorial article). DocuSign ties shallow depth of field to subjects standing out clearly, and stroke overlays to its principles of connection and dynamism.

## Where the reason sits on the page (fact for the read sources)

- TikTok: one line in the left panel next to the rule, plus one derivation page. No separate "why" chapter.
- FIFTHSIP on Behance: a project description (problem, creative direction, solution) and a caption under each visual.
- BMA and Amazing Thailand: an article or press text that walks element by element; the guideline itself was not seen.
- DocuSign: one short clause inside the rule text.

## Pattern (inference, below 0.7)

The stronger examples give every element a reason of one of these kinds and keep it next to the element, so the reader never leaves the page to learn why. Thai public identities lean on cultural meaning (kind 2) more than the global tech brands, which lean on derivation and function (kinds 1 and 3).

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

ORIVILO's Intent Engine keeps every artifact traceable to its source reasons. These five kinds are a ready vocabulary for the reason attached to each guidebook element, and the placement (one line beside the element, or a caption) shows how a generated guidebook could display that trace.
