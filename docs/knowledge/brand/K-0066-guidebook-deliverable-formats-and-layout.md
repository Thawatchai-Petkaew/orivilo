## Evidence

> visually striking, thoughtfully conceived, and executed with skill and precision

(Behance Team, "How to Get Featured on Behance", 14 August 2025, the curators' criteria, as returned by the fetch)

## Paged book (fact)

- Page counts seen: TikTok For Business 70 (16:9 slides), Discord 2021 74, Burger King partners 2020 129 (by Jones Knowles Ritchie), Gojek 200+ (via search summary), NASA Graphics Standards Manual reissue 220.
- Layout inside the TikTok book: numbered black section dividers, a breadcrumb on every page, a rule column and a visual column, numbered misuse pages, values printed under swatches and type levels, download buttons that point to files outside the book, and "WIP" stamps on unfinished pages (details in [[K-0061-tiktok-guidelines-page-layout-and-reasons]]).
- An archive, brandingstyleguides.com, collects such books (its letter index adds up to roughly 3,900 entries, by our sum of its counts) and lets readers filter by year, page count, region and tag; documents are "provided by brands, designers, or submitted by members for educational purposes".

## Web brand site (fact)

- DocuSign's site has one page per element in its navigation (Logo, Color, Typography, Elements, Sound, Photography, Motion, Video, Product, Resources) and holds media a PDF cannot, such as the 3-second sonic logo and a ten-track playlist.
- Grab keeps a partner-facing brand centre per sub-brand (GrabFood, GrabMart) on its merchant site (via search summary). Uber's brand address now redirects to an assets site (seen as a 307 redirect; the page body was not readable).

## Behance case study (fact for the curators' rules; the case-study order from one editorial article)

- Curators judge visual impact, concept, execution, presentation, context and community traction, and review a project once, at first publication.
- Presentation advice: images 1400px wide, a cover with no text that shows the project's look and feel, 5 to 15 images, an optional write-up on scope, client, process and inspiration.
- The FIFTHSIP project, featured in Behance's own blog, moves from brand introduction to design elements to real-world applications, with captions that explain each decision.

## Pattern (inference, below 0.7)

The usage book is for the people who apply the brand (rules, values, misuse); the case study is for the people who judge or buy it (story, concept, applications in context). The owner's sense that Behance shows "better mood" fits this: case studies lead with applications and atmosphere, while usage books lead with rules. A guidebook that opens with the case-study flow (story, concept, mood in use) and then gives the usage rules would serve both readers.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

ORV-126 must specify the guidebook as an output. This entry lays out the formats in use, their typical length and layout, and what the showcase format adds, so the designer and architect can choose the output shape knowing the options.
