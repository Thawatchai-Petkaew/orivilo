## Evidence

Read in a browser on 2026-10-26, screenshot on file (session scratchpad, `behance/t/` and `behance/owner-t/`; not added to the repo). Behance refuses a direct fetch, so the gallery URLs are link-only. The researcher read at least the first tile of all 18 projects; later tiles were read only where listed in [[K-0062-brand-guideline-projects-studied]].

> Letter E + Sun + Home = Evolta Solar

(Evolta, tile 00, the captions under four icons joined by plus and equals signs)

## The six, by form (fact)

Equation of parts, drawn as icons in a row:

- Evolta (solar). Letter E + sun + home = the mark. The overview text repeats it in words.
- Truway (travel). Letter T + pathway + arrow icon + tree icon = brand mark.
- Sit (meditation). Pinecone + eye + a third small icon = the mark; the third label was too small to read.
- Leadeep (solar). A "Meaning of the logo" page: sun icon + solar panel grid, each shown beside a source photo, with a short paragraph.

Paragraph only:

- Jorixent (architectural visualisation). The mark hides the letters J and r, built from quarter ellipses.
- True Money Wallet (Bangkok). A simplified wallet with cards folding out that also forms a W for wallet, and can fold out when animated.

Not counted: Hedwig shows the owl mark on a construction grid but no stated reason on the tiles; the orchestrator's note reads an H hidden in the owl, which the researcher could not confirm at tile resolution. Kryon, Mose and Green Lead show construction or structure grids without a meaning. Aspot's contents list a "Logo meaning" page that was not on the tile read.

## Where the parts come from (fact, then inference)

- Letter and meaning symbol together: Evolta, Truway, True Money (three; four if Hedwig's H is confirmed).
- Meaning symbols only: Sit, Leadeep.
- Letters only: Jorixent.

Inference, 0.7: the equation form is the clearest way seen to show a logo's reason, because each part is named and the sum is the mark, and it was seen in four projects. Inference, 0.55: "a letter from the name plus a symbol from the meaning" is one common recipe, not the rule; it was seen in three of six.

## Why it matters for ORIVILO

An equation is a trace in visual form: each part of the mark points to a source (the name, or a meaning from the brief). ORIVILO could show a generated logo's reason the same way, with each part linked to the intent it came from. See also [[K-0065-how-guidelines-show-each-elements-reason]].
